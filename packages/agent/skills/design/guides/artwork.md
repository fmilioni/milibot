# Logos, marks and illustrations (`design_draw`)

## Sizes

- Logo mark (symbol): 512×512.
- Wordmark or lockup: don't draw the letters. Draw the mark, then set the name next to it in HTML with the design's font (`<div class="flex items-center gap-3"><img data-art="Mark" class="size-10"><span class="font-display text-2xl">Acme</span></div>`).
- Spot illustration (empty state, feature): 800×600.
- Flat illustration the user asked for: 1440×800, or the shape of the space it fills.

## Writing the brief

Say everything; the drawer sees nothing of the design:

- the subject and what it should convey;
- the composition and framing (centered symbol, subject on the left, full-bleed scene);
- the style: flat or line art, stroke weight, rounded or sharp corners, geometric or organic, level of detail;
- the exact colors as hex from your tokens, and which one dominates;
- the background: transparent unless the art needs its own;
- what to avoid (text, gradients, realism, clutter).

Examples:

- "Logo mark for Tidal, a marketing agency: a stylized wave curling into a circle, bold geometric shapes, flat, no outlines, no text. Colors #153F39 (main) and #C6F24E (one accent stroke). Transparent background, centered, works at 32 px."
- "Mascot for a kids' reading app: a friendly round owl reading an open book, front view, flat shapes with soft rounded corners, minimal detail. Colors #2B59C3, #F2B134, #FFFFFF, dark details #1A1A2E. Transparent background, centered."
- "Empty-state illustration for an inbox with no messages: an open envelope with a small paper plane leaving it, line art with 3 px strokes, rounded caps. Stroke #6B7280, one accent #0EA5E9. Transparent background, lots of air around it."
- "Flat hero illustration for a budgeting app (the user asked for flat art): a stack of coins turning into a rising bar chart with an upward arrow, geometric, subject on the right third, left side calm for the headline. Colors #0F172A, #22C55E, #E2E8F0, #F8FAFC. Background #F8FAFC."
- "Seamless-looking decorative pattern for a footer band: scattered small leaves and dots, flat, low contrast. Colors #D1FAE5 and #A7F3D0 on transparent."
