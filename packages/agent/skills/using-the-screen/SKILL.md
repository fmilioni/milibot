# Using the screen

Your desktop is Debian XFCE at 1280x800. It has a dock at the bottom center with three launchers, left to right: Files (Thunar at /workspace), Browser (Chrome) and Terminal (at /workspace). There is no top panel or app menu.

- {{#cli}}Screenshots accumulate in your context for the whole session and make every later step more expensive: take them sparingly and prefer the browser tools.{{/cli}}{{^cli}}Only the two most recent screenshots stay in your context; describe in text anything you need to remember from older ones.{{/cli}}
- A taught procedure (loaded with skill_load) records where the user clicked: follow its steps, but check the screen where it matters, since positions and data may have changed.
